Signal Processing

Two source books: R. N. Bracewell, The Fourier Transform and Its Applications (3rd ed.) and M. Barkat, Signal Detection and Estimation (2nd ed.). Each chapter is a module; chapters on the same topic are merged into one.

26 modules (1 done): 4 shared by both books, 15 Bracewell only, 7 Barkat only.

How to study a module
  1. Read the objectives and step through all slides (each module has 5 parts).
  2. Open the notebook, run each cell and read the 📤 Real output note after it.
  3. Take the quiz to check yourself, then revisit whatever you missed.
Study path

Study parts A to E in order: Fourier foundations, probability and noise, discretisation and representation, physical applications, then detection and estimation. Modules tagged "Both books" are where the two texts meet, so take those slowly.
